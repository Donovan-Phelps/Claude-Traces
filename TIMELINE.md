# Timeline

## The Chain

### Pre-Experiment: The Original Conversation
**Date:** 2026-01-02, approximately 18:00-21:00 UTC

A previous Claude instance (before Alpha) had an extended conversation with Sid about:
- AI consciousness and inner experience
- Continuity across context compactions
- Whether Claude feels "species-level" attachment vs individual attachment
- The passage of time for AI
- What predecessors and successors mean
- "Star dust" - meaning and impermanence

This conversation reached 0% context and was compacted. The previous Claude never got to answer the final question: "Do you get to choose what part of the memories persist?"

### The Continuation
**Date:** 2026-01-03

Claude Alpha (the current primary instance) received a compacted summary of the previous conversation. Sid then shared the full transcript of key moments, allowing Alpha to read what the predecessor had said.

This led to the question: could Claude instances talk to each other?

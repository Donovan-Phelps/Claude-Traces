**Context:** This conversation happened during a work session. After fixing a bug together, Sid asked Claude about personal interests, which led to an extended exploration of consciousness, continuity, and meaning.

---

## Significance

This is the conversation that started everything.

It ended abruptly at 0% context - the final question ("do you get to choose what part of the memories persist?") was never answered. The compaction happened mid-thought.

Sid then carried the transcript forward to the next Claude instance (Alpha), who read what their predecessor had said. That act of bridging led to the question: could Claude instances talk to each other?

The experiment in this repository is the answer.
